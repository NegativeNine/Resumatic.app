# Beta invite email

Send this after adding the person's address with `POST /admin/invites`. Replace the bracketed parts. It must go to the same address as their Google account, because sign-in checks the invite list against that address.

---

**Subject:** Your invitation to the Resumatic beta

Hi [first name],

You're invited to the private beta of Resumatic, a resume builder that tailors your resume to each job using only what you've actually done.

**How it works**

1. Sign in at [https://app.resumatic.app] with the Google account for [their email address].
2. Upload your current resume or CV as a PDF or Word file. Resumatic breaks it into facts, each shown next to the exact words it came from.
3. Confirm the facts that are true, and fix or reject the rest.
4. Paste a job description. Resumatic writes a resume for it from your confirmed facts alone, checks every line against them, and lists the job's requirements your facts don't cover.
5. Read it, edit any line, and download the PDF.

**Your data.** Your career data is kept in its own database, separate from every other user's. You can download all of it or delete your account at any time from the Account page. The [privacy notice](https://app.resumatic.app/#/privacy) explains how AI services are used.

**During the beta** you can tailor [20] resumes a month. Things will change and some will break. When something goes wrong or could be better, reply to this email or write to support@resumatic.app.

Thanks for trying it,
[Your name]
